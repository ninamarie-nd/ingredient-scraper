Scan a product label

Take a photo of the ingredient list and we will grade the product. Photos and privacy: the photo is sent to Google Cloud, which reads the text. Google processes the photo in memory, does not store it, and does not use it to train its models. Our site does not keep your photo. Google keeps basic request details, such as the time and size of the request, to run the service and prevent abuse.

Drag the blue edges to frame just the ingredient list, or use the full photo.
