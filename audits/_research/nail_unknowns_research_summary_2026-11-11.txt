## How the requested fields are handled

### Classification and endocrine evidence are separate

The workbook separates:

1. IARC classification.
2. NTP Report on Carcinogens listing.
3. NTP experimental findings.
4. California Proposition 65 listing.
5. Enacted EU harmonized CMR classification.
6. Proposals, RAC opinions, supplier classifications, and other toxicology findings.

A reproductive-toxicity finding is not automatically evidence of an endocrine mechanism. Similarly, an NTP animal study is not automatically an NTP Report on Carcinogens listing.

### Feedstock cannot usually be determined from INCI

“Synthetic” does not establish “petroleum-derived.” Where a material can be manufactured by different routes, the grade-specific feedstock is recorded as “no data found.” Cellulose-derived ingredients, licorice-derived glycyrrhizate, rosin, and diatomaceous earth have identifiable biological or geological starting materials, but that does not establish the provenance of every reagent or commercial grade. [1117] [cir-safety](https://www.cir-safety.org/sites/default/files/nitroc062013FR.pdf)

### PFAS result

For the defined identities below, the structural screen is No: none contains an identified OECD PFAS moiety. OECD’s definition requires at least one fully fluorinated methyl or methylene carbon, subject to its stated qualifications. Silicon, sulfur, phosphate, “fluorescein,” and “polymer” do not themselves establish PFAS identity. [oecd](https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/publications/reports/2021/07/reconciling-terminology-of-the-universe-of-per-and-polyfluoroalkyl-substances_a7fbcba8/e458e796-en.pdf)

This is not an analytical PFAS-free determination. Commercial surface treatments, impurities, and inadequately characterized polymer grades remain unknown.

### Red 17: legal listing and unresolved scientific concern coexist

The 2005 SCCP opinion states that CI 26100 can release 4-aminoazobenzene through azo cleavage and concludes that the submitted data did not allow assessment of consumer risk. Its reference to “carcinogen category 2” concerns the cleavage product under the historical pre-CLP system—not current CLP Carc. 2 assigned to Sudan III. [ec.europa](https://ec.europa.eu/health/ph_risk/committees/04_sccp/docs/sccp_o_013.pdf)

That finding must remain separate from Sudan III’s IARC Group 3 classification. Group 3 means not classifiable, not demonstrated noncarcinogenicity. [1118][1126]
