# ATTITUDE Kids Audit — Content Mining Report

**Date audited:** 2026-07-09
**Products audited:** 11 products / 32 scent variants
**Source:** https://attitudeliving.com (Baby + Kids → Kids)

---

## Section A — Brand-level callout

**Headline finding:** Of 32 ATTITUDE Kids variants audited across 11 products, exactly **1 is Puberty Safe** (the Unscented Mineral Sunscreen Stick). Every scented product grades **Avoid (Moderate)** or worse — and the two Mineral Sunscreen *lotions* grade **Avoid (High Priority Swap)**.

**Tier distribution:**

- Puberty Safe: 1 (3%)
- Use Caution: 0 (0%)
- Avoid (Mild): 0 (0%)
- Avoid (Moderate): 29 (91%)
- Avoid (High Priority Swap): 2 (6%)

**Single most-flagged / most-concerning product:** Kids Mineral Face & Body Sunscreen SPF 50 (lotion), Watermelon & Coco — 5 flags and the only tier above Moderate. It stacks Propanediol [4], a quaternary compound (Stearalkonium Hectorite) [2], plain Silica [1,2] carrying a "PFAS-treated" watch-note, Vanillin [4,8], and a "Fragrance (Parfum)" catch-all [1,2,4,8]. The 2-in-1 Wash and Bubble Bath (Blueberry / Watermelon scents) tie for the highest cosmetic flag count at 5.

**Cleanest product:** Kids Mineral Sunscreen Stick SPF 50 — Unscented — Puberty Safe, 0 flags. It is essentially coconut oil, sunflower/grape-seed oils, shea butter, non-nano zinc oxide, and tocopherol. It is the *only* clean product in the entire kids line.

**Content angle for Section A:** "I scanned all 32 versions of ATTITUDE's Kids line — the EWG-Verified brand parents trust — and only ONE came back clean. It wasn't a shampoo, a bubble bath, or a lotion. It was a single unscented sunscreen stick." The gap between the brand's clean-label positioning and a 91%-Avoid result is the spine of the post.

## Section B — Pattern callouts

**Most common flagged ingredient:** the fragrance/flavour catch-all — "Fragrance (Parfum)" appears in 25 variants and "Flavour (Aroma)" in 5, so **30 of 32 variants (94%) carry an undisclosed-remainder fragrance/flavour line** [risk 1,2,4,8]. The only two without it are the two Unscented sunscreens.

**Second most common:** Sodium Coco-Sulfate [4,5], a sulfate surfactant, in **12 variants** — every scent of the 2-in-1 Wash, Bubble Bath, and Foaming Hand Soap.

**Most common risk category:** Categories 1 (Carcinogen), 2 (Endocrine Disruption), and 4 (Allergies/Irritation) are each represented in **31 of 32 variants**, and Category 8 (Respiratory) in 30 — all driven primarily by the fragrance/flavour catch-all.

**"Clean marketing, dirty formula" finds:** every single product carries **EWG VERIFIED, VEGAN, and DERMATOLOGICALLY TESTED** badges on its page, yet:

- The Kids Mineral Sunscreen *lotion* (both scents) is EWG Verified but pulls **Avoid (High Priority Swap)** — plain Silica in the formula carries a "PFAS-treated" watch-note in the reference, on a product applied liberally to kids' skin.
- Every "gentle 2-in-1 kids" shampoo pairs a sulfate (Sodium Coco-Sulfate) with a synthetic fragrance blend.
- ATTITUDE actually *discloses* its individual fragrance chemicals (unusually transparent) — which is how we can see they are lab molecules like 2-T-Butylcyclohexyl Acetate, Hexahydro-Methanoindenyl Propionate, and Raspberry Ketone, not the "naturally derived" impression the marketing gives.

**Repeated naming patterns:**

- Every wash-type product (2-in-1 Wash, Bubble Bath, Foaming Hand Soap = 12 of 12 variants) contains **Sodium Coco-Sulfate**.
- Every scented product (30 of 32) ends its ingredient list with a synthetic fragrance/flavour tail plus a **"Fragrance (Parfum)" / "Flavour (Aroma)"** wrapper — even though individual components are named.
- The line uses **27 distinct synthetic fragrance/flavour chemicals** not present in the reference database (full list in Raw Data Summary).

**Content angles for Section B:**

1. "ATTITUDE lists its fragrance chemicals — so let's read them. Here are the actual lab molecules hiding behind 'fruity fragrance' in your kid's shampoo." (Transparency turned against the marketing.)
2. "Same three badges on every product — EWG Verified, Vegan, Dermatologically Tested — and 30 of 32 still contain a synthetic fragrance blend." (Badge ≠ fragrance-free.)
3. "'Gentle 2-in-1 kids wash' + sulfate. Every scent. Every time." (Sulfate-in-kids-shampoo angle.)

## Section C — Comparison angles

**Best vs worst within categories:**

- Sunscreen: Best = Mineral Sunscreen Stick, Unscented (**Puberty Safe**). Worst = Mineral Sunscreen *lotion*, Watermelon & Coco (**Avoid — High Priority Swap**). Same brand, same SPF 50, same "kids mineral" promise — opposite ends of the scale.
- Bath/Body cleansers: Best = Kids Conditioner, Vanilla & Pear / Mango (Moderate, 1 flag — fragrance only). Worst = 2-in-1 Wash & Bubble Bath, Blueberry/Watermelon (Moderate, 5 flags — sulfate + isethionate + quat + fragrance chemical + fragrance).
- Toothpaste: Best = Fluoride-Free, Mango (Moderate, 2 flags). Worst = Fluoride, Watermelon (Moderate, 3 flags — adds Sodium Lauryl Sulfate).

**Similar marketing, different grades:**

- The two sunscreens read almost identically on-page ("kids mineral SPF 50, EWG Verified") but the **stick is the cleanest product in the line and the lotion is the dirtiest.** Format, not marketing, drives the grade — the lotion adds Silica, Propanediol, and a quaternary thickener the stick doesn't need.
- Fluoride vs Fluoride-Free toothpaste look like a "safety" choice, but both still land at Avoid (Moderate) on flavour chemistry; the only real delta is **Sodium Lauryl Sulfate**, present in the fluoride version and absent from the fluoride-free.

**Cleanest in each category:**

- Sunscreen: Mineral Sunscreen Stick, Unscented — Puberty Safe.
- Bath/Body: Kids Conditioner (Vanilla & Pear or Mango) — Moderate, 1 flag (fragrance only), no sulfate.
- Toothpaste: Fluoride-Free, Mango — Moderate, 2 flags, no SLS.
- Hand: Hand Sanitizer (any scent) — Moderate, 1 flag (fragrance only); milder base than the Foaming Hand Soap, which adds Sodium Coco-Sulfate.

**Content angles for Section C:**

1. "Two ATTITUDE kids sunscreens. Same SPF. Same EWG badge. One's the cleanest thing they make; the other is the only High-Priority-Swap in the line. The difference? Stick vs lotion." (Format-matters comparison.)
2. "Fluoride-free toothpaste feels like the 'clean' pick — but ATTITUDE's fluoride version's real problem isn't fluoride, it's the sulfate (SLS) they slipped in." (Reframing the toothpaste decision.)
3. "Want the least-bad ATTITUDE kids wash? Skip the 2-in-1 and grab the conditioner — it drops the sulfate and the second surfactant. Still fragranced, though." (Trade-up-within-brand angle.)

## Raw data summary

**Unknown ingredients across the audit (manual review queue) — 27 unique, all disclosed synthetic fragrance/flavour components not in the reference database:**

1,2-Dihydrolinalool; 2,6-Dimethyl-7-Octen-2-Ol; 2-T-Butylcyclohexyl Acetate; 3-Hexenol; 3-Hexyl Hexanoate; Alpha-Ionone; Butyl Isovalerate; Dimethyl Heptenal; Ethyl 2-Methylbutyrate; Ethyl Butyrate; Ethyl Hexanoate; Gamma-Decalactone; Gamma-Nonalactone; Gamma-Octalactone; Gamma-Undecalactone; Hexahydro-Methanoindenyl Propionate; Hexenol; Hexenyl Acetate; Hexyl Acetate; Hexyl Hexanoate; Isoamyl Acetate; L-Alpha-Pinene; Linalyl Acetate; Maltol; Methyldihydrojasmonate; Raspberry Ketone; Terpineol.

These are candidates to add to the reference database; several (e.g., Linalyl Acetate, Terpineol, Alpha-Ionone) are known fragrance allergens/sensitizers and would likely carry Category 4 flags once classified.

**Grading-interpretation note (applies to nearly every row):** ATTITUDE discloses individual fragrance/flavour components but still prints a "*Fragrance (Parfum)" / "*Flavour (Aroma)" catch-all, indicating an undisclosed remainder. This audit treats that catch-all as a full fragrance flag [1,2,4,8] → minimum tier Avoid (Moderate). If Nina-Marie prefers to credit the partial disclosure as a "soft" flag, most scented products would drop toward Use Caution / Avoid (Mild); the two sunscreen lotions would remain High Priority (PFAS-silica) and the washes would remain Avoid on the sulfate rule.

**Reformulation / anomaly flags:** none observed. Minor source-copy quirks preserved verbatim: the Mango 2-in-1 Wash lists "Vaccinium Mytillus" (brand typo for Myrtillus) and a missing space at "Isethionate,Lauryl"; the Watermelon Conditioner prints "*fragrance" lowercase. These do not affect grading.
