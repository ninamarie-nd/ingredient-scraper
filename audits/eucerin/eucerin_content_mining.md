# Eucerin (US) Audit — Content Mining Report

**Date audited:** 2026-10-16
**Products audited:** 48
**Source:** https://www.eucerinus.com/products

**Important caveat:** No Eucerin product page publishes a full INCI list. Each page prints only key ingredients (Ingredients block) or active ingredients (sunscreens, oatmeal products). Every tier below describes the printed subset only and is provisional. All 48 rows are marked "needs review."

---

## Section A — Brand-level callout

**Headline finding:** Of 48 Eucerin US products audited, 27 grade Puberty Safe on the ingredients the site prints, and every product page omits the full formula.

**Tier distribution:**
- Puberty Safe: 27 (56%)
- Use Caution: 6 (13%)
- Avoid (Mild): 10 (21%)
- Avoid (Moderate): 0 (0%)
- Avoid (High Priority Swap): 5 (10%)

**Single most-flagged product:** Tie at 4 flags, all sunscreens: Daily Lotion SPF 30, Advanced Hydration Ultra-Light SPF 50 Face Sunscreen, Advanced Hydration SPF 50 Sunscreen Spray, Advanced Hydration SPF 50 Sunscreen, Clear Skin SPF 50 Face Sunscreen, Age Defense SPF 50 Face Sunscreen, Tinted Age Defense SPF 50 Face Sunscreen, Advanced Hydration SPF 30 Sunscreen Spray. Each lists four chemical UV filters, all in reference category 2 (endocrine disruption).

**Cleanest product (if any):** 27 products tie at Puberty Safe with zero flagged ingredients on the printed list. The mineral sunscreens (Sensitive Mineral SPF 50 Sunscreen, Baby Sensitive Mineral SPF 50 Sunscreen Lotion, Tinted Sensitive Mineral SPF 35 Face Sunscreen) print only zinc oxide, which is LOW TOX in lotions.

**Content angle for Section A:**
"I scanned every Eucerin product on the US site. The site prints only a few key ingredients per product, so the grades are a floor, not a full read." Pair the 27-of-48 headline with the partial-list caveat.

## Section B — Pattern callouts

**Most common flagged ingredient:** Octisalate — appears in 9 of 48 products (printed list). Avobenzone, Homosalate and Octocrylene each appear in 8 of 48.

**Most common risk category:** Category 2 (Endocrine Disruption) — flagged in 11 products (9 sunscreens with chemical UV filters, plus 2 serums that print Butylene Glycol, which the reference lists as categories 1 and 2).

**"Clean marketing, dirty formula" finds:**
- Eucerin Advanced Cleansing Body and Face Wash: marketed as fragrance free, dye free and paraben free. The printed list shows Sodium Myreth Sulfate (reference-inferred categories 1, 4, 5), which puts it at Avoid (Mild).
- Baby Wash and Shampoo: a baby product for sensitive skin. The printed list shows Sodium Myreth Sulfate, which puts it at Avoid (Mild).
- Original Healing Rich Cream and Original Healing Lotion: marketed for extremely dry, compromised skin. The printed lists show Mineral Oil and Petrolatum, both reference category 1. Mineral oil or petrolatum in a leave-on product triggers the High Priority Swap escalation.
- The chemical-filter sunscreens are sold as sun protection, several tagged "Dry skin" or "Acne prone skin" on the site. Each prints four chemical UV filters, all reference category 2, which puts them at Avoid (Mild).

**Repeated naming patterns:**
- The same printed ingredient text appears on four products: "Water, Glycerin, Urea" on Advanced Repair Cream 16 oz, Advanced Repair Lotion, Advanced Repair Hand Cream, and Advanced Repair Cream Tube 8 oz. That suggests the site prints a shared key-ingredient line, not each SKU's formula.
- Every chemical-filter sunscreen (8 of 8) prints the same four UV filters. Only the percentages differ.
- Colloidal oatmeal appears as the only printed ingredient on 7 eczema and baby products. The reference has no entry for it, so each is unscored.

**Content angles for Section B:**
1. "Eucerin's sunscreens look interchangeable, but the chemical-filter lineup prints the same four UV filters in every SPF 30 and SPF 50 product." Use the 8-product Avoid (Mild) run as the spine.
2. "Dry-skin creams that print only three ingredients can still carry mineral oil or petrolatum." Use Original Healing as the example. Frame as what the reference flags, not a product claim.
3. "Two grades can come from one ingredient." Sodium Myreth Sulfate drives Avoid (Mild) in both the cleanser and the baby wash.

## Section C — Comparison angles

**Best vs worst within categories:**
- Cleansers: Best = Hydrating Cleansing Gel, 13.5 FL OZ (Puberty Safe; printed Water, Glycerin, Coco-Betaine). Worst = Eucerin Advanced Cleansing Body and Face Wash (Avoid (Mild); Sodium Myreth Sulfate).
- Creams and lotions: Best = Puberty Safe items such as Advanced Repair Cream and Skin Calming Cream (zero flags on printed list). Worst = Original Healing Rich Cream (High Priority Swap; Petrolatum, Mineral Oil).
- Serums: Dual Serum and Moisture Boost Serum both carry 2 flags (Alcohol Denat. and Butylene Glycol, reference categories 1 and 2) and both reach High Priority Swap through the leave-on category 1 escalation. There is no cleaner serum in the catalog.
- Sunscreens: Best = Sensitive Mineral SPF 50 Sunscreen (Puberty Safe; zinc oxide only). Worst = eight-way tie at Avoid (Mild), 4 chemical filters each.
- Body and eczema care: Best = Eczema Relief Cream and similar oatmeal products (Puberty Safe on printed text; colloidal oatmeal unscored). Worst = Baby Wash and Shampoo (Avoid (Mild)).

**Similar marketing, different grades:**
- Sensitive Mineral SPF 50 Sunscreen (Puberty Safe; zinc oxide only) vs Advanced Hydration SPF 50 Sunscreen (Avoid (Mild); four chemical filters). Both are SPF 50 sunscreens on the same site, and the Advanced Hydration version is tagged "Dry skin."
- Baby Sensitive Mineral SPF 50 Sunscreen Lotion (Puberty Safe) vs Advanced Hydration SPF 50 Sunscreen Spray (Avoid (Mild)). Both are SPF 50 sun products sold to families with sensitive-skin positioning on the mineral line.

**Cleanest in each category:**
- Cleansers: Hydrating Cleansing Gel, 13.5 FL OZ (Puberty Safe on printed text; two unknowns, Gluco-Glycerol and APG Complex, appear on the 6.8 FL OZ size).
- Creams and lotions: Advanced Repair Cream 16 oz (Puberty Safe on printed text; no unknowns).
- Sunscreens: Sensitive Mineral SPF 50 Sunscreen (Puberty Safe on printed text).
- Oatmeal and eczema: several Puberty Safe on printed text, but colloidal oatmeal is unscored.

**Content angles for Section C:**
1. "Same brand, same SPF 50, opposite grades: mineral versus chemical filters." Use the Sensitive Mineral vs Advanced Hydration SPF 50 pair.
2. "Two cleansers from one brand, two grades." Use Hydrating Cleansing Gel vs Advanced Cleansing Body and Face Wash, with the sulfate as the reason.
3. "Eczema-line creams score clean on the printed text, but the printed text is only one oatmeal line." Use to explain why the partial-list caveat matters.
