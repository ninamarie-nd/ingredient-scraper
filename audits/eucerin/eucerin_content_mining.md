# Eucerin (US) Audit: Content Mining Report

**Date audited:** 2026-10-16
**Products:** 48 (catalog at https://www.eucerinus.com/products)
**Verified full ingredient lists:** 35 (retailer pages: Walmart 31, Target 1, Amazon 3)
**Provisional rows:** 13 (conflicts, unconfirmed size, candidate-only text, or no text found)

**Caveat for every number below:** Grades use retailer-verified INCI lists where they exist. The 13 provisional rows are graded from the brand-site subset and are marked "needs review." Grades are not final until those rows are resolved.

## Section A: Brand-level callout

**Headline finding:** Of 35 Eucerin products with a verified full ingredient list, 28 grade Avoid (High Priority Swap) and 2 grade Puberty Safe. The main driver is phenoxyethanol, a reference category 1 ingredient, in leave-on products.

**Tier distribution, verified 35:**
- Puberty Safe: 2 (6%)
- Use Caution: 2 (6%)
- Avoid (Mild): 3 (9%)
- Avoid (Moderate): 0 (0%)
- Avoid (High Priority Swap): 28 (80%)

**Tier distribution, all 48 (includes provisional rows):**
- Puberty Safe: 8
- Use Caution: 4
- Avoid (Mild): 5
- Avoid (Moderate): 0
- Avoid (High Priority Swap): 31

The earlier brand-site-only pass graded 27 products Puberty Safe. That count was based on the partial printed subset and is superseded.

**Single most-flagged products:** Tinted Age Defense SPF 50 Face Sunscreen (13 flags), Clear Skin SPF 50 Face Sunscreen (11), Age Defense SPF 50 Face Sunscreen (11), Tinted Sensitive Mineral SPF 35 Face Sunscreen (10).

**Cleanest verified product:** Hydrating Cleansing Gel. Both verified sizes (13.5 fl oz on Walmart, 6.8 fl oz on Amazon) grade Puberty Safe with zero flagged ingredients.

**Content angle for Section A:** "I checked the full ingredient lists for 35 Eucerin products on retailer sites. Most leave-on products carried a reference category 1 preservative." Pair the 28-of-35 figure with the sourcing note, and say which products are still pending.

## Section B: Pattern callouts

**Phenoxyethanol drives most tiers.** Phenoxyethanol (reference categories 1, 2, 4, 5, 8) appears in 25 of 48 rows. In a leave-on product, a category 1 ingredient triggers the High Priority Swap rule. Nine High Priority Swap rows reach that tier only through this rule.

**Category frequency, verified 35:**
- Category 4 in 33 products
- Category 2 in 31
- Category 1 in 31
- Category 5 in 29
- Category 8 in 24
- Category 6 in 17
- Category 3 in 4

**Sunscreens are the most-flagged group.** Chemical-filter SPF products carry multiple reference category 2 UV filters, plus phenoxyethanol or similar preservatives, which pushes them to the top of the flag counts.

**"Marketing versus formula" angles to verify before publishing:**
- Sensitive and mineral-branded sunscreens are not automatically clean. Check the verified list for each product before pairing it with a mineral claim.
- Dry-skin and eczema-line creams can carry the same preservative stack as the sunscreens. Confirm each product's verified list first.

**Content angles for Section B:**
1. "Most Eucerin leave-on products carry the same preservative." Use phenoxyethanol as the single driver, and explain the rule in one sentence.
2. "The sunscreen line has the most flags per product." Use the Tinted Age Defense SPF 50 Face Sunscreen as the example.
3. "A short ingredient list is not a clean list." Use the 13 provisional rows to show why the full INCI matters.

## Section C: Comparison angles

**Cleanest in the verified set:** Hydrating Cleansing Gel (Puberty Safe, 0 flags), in both sizes.

**Worst in the verified set:** the sunscreen group. The four most-flagged products are all verified sunscreens, with 10 to 13 flags each.

**Within-category contrasts (verified rows only):**
- Cleansers: Hydrating Cleansing Gel (Puberty Safe) versus Eucerin Advanced Cleansing Body and Face Wash (Avoid (Mild)).
- Sunscreens: the verified SPF 50 face and body sunscreens all grade Avoid (High Priority Swap). The Advanced Hydration SPF 30 Sunscreen Spray grades Avoid (Mild), but it is provisional and is not in the verified set.

**Content angles for Section C:**
1. "Two cleansers from one brand, two grades." Use the Hydrating Cleansing Gel versus the Advanced Cleansing Body and Face Wash, with the surfactant as the reason.
2. "Same brand, same SPF, different flag counts." Use the Advanced Hydration SPF 30 spray and the SPF 50 face sunscreens.
