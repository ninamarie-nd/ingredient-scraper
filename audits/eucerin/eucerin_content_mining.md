# Eucerin (US) Audit — Content Mining Report

**Date audited:** 2026-10-16 (regraded 2026-10-17 under practitioner decisions D1 to D8 and change 3: five Use Caution products moved to ungradable)
**Products audited:** 48 listed: 3 graded, 45 ungradable (40 with two or more unknown ingredients, 5 by the change 3 exception), 0 without ingredient text
**Source:** https://www.eucerinus.com/ (product names and retailer-printed ingredient lists; sources per row in the audit log)

**Read this first:** "Ungradable" is not a grade. It means two or more printed ingredients are not in the reference (D4 and D7), so no tier is assigned. A product with exactly one such ingredient gets a Use Caution floor, or keeps a higher tier from its other flags (D8). Partial flags on ungradable products are for ranking only. The three graded products are provisional; see Section A.

## Section A — Brand-level callout

**Headline finding:** Of 48 Eucerin products, 3 can be graded: 2 Avoid (Mild) and 1 Avoid (High Priority Swap). The other 45 are ungradable: 40 carry two or more ingredients the reference does not cover, and 5 were moved to ungradable under change 3 (2026-10-17) because their grade rested on printed text the audit marks as unresolved. None of the 48 is now Use Caution. The two Avoid (Mild) grades are provisional because their audit status is needs review.

**Tier distribution (48 products):**
- Puberty Safe: 0 (0%)
- Use Caution: 0 (0%)
- Avoid (Mild): 2 (4%)
- Avoid (Moderate): 0 (0%)
- Avoid (High Priority Swap): 1 (2%)
- Ungradable, counted separately: 45 (94%), of which 40 carry two or more unknown ingredients (D7), 5 were moved to ungradable by the change 3 exception, and 0 have no ingredient text

**Single most-flagged product:** Tinted Age Defense SPF 50 Face Sunscreen — 13 partial flags, ungradable. The most-flagged graded products carry 4 flags each: Advanced Hydration SPF 30 Sunscreen Spray (Avoid (Mild), graded from four listed actives only) and Moisture Boost Serum (Avoid (High Priority Swap), escalated by a category 1 flag in a leave-on, with one unknown).

**Cleanest product (if any):** Baby Wash and Shampoo — Avoid (Mild), 1 flag (Sodium Myreth Sulfate, sulfate pattern), provisional (audit status needs review). The zero-flag products, Q10 Revitalize Night Cream and Baby Lotion, are ungradable under change 3 because their printed text is one name or unresolved, so they are not clean results.

## Section B — Pattern callouts

**Most common flagged ingredient:** Phenoxyethanol — flagged in 26 of 48 products (reference categories 1, 2, 4, 5, 8). Next: Dimethicone (15 products), Alcohol Denat (14), Ethylhexylglycerin (12).

**Most common risk category:** Category 4 (Allergies, Irritation, Corrosion) — flagged in 38 of 48 products. Category 2 (Endocrine Disruption) appears in 37 and Category 1 (Carcinogen) in 36. These counts include partial flags on ungradable products.

**"Clean marketing, dirty formula" finds:**
- Sensitive Mineral SPF 50 Sunscreen and Baby Sensitive Mineral SPF 50 Sunscreen Lotion. The product names say "Mineral." Each has 7 partial flags (Dimethicone, Cyclopentasiloxane, PEG-12 Dimethicone Crosspolymer, Propylene Glycol, 1,2-Hexanediol, Butyloctyl Salicylate, Cetyl PEG/PPG-10/1 Dimethicone) and 8 unknowns.
- Eczema Relief Flare-Up Treatment has 7 partial flags (Mineral Oil, PEG-40 Stearate, Decylene Glycol, 1,2-Hexanediol, Phenoxyethanol, Ethylhexylglycerin, BHT). The Eucerin Eczema Relief Hydrogel in the same line has 1.
- Baby Wash and Shampoo grades Avoid (Mild) on one flag (Sodium Myreth Sulfate, sulfate pattern). Provisional, see Section A.

**Repeated naming patterns:**
- Phenoxyethanol appears in 26 of 48 products.
- Silica appears in 6 SPF products. Under D1 its PFAS-treated tag does not escalate; its reference categories [1, 2] still count.
- Iron oxides appear in 3 products. Under D2 they carry category 3 (heavy metals) and the PFAS tag, which escalates the grade whenever they are present.

**Naming variant to decide:** Silica Dimethyl Silylate (5 products) is unknown. It is not the reference's "Silica Silylate" entry (categories 1 and 2, PFAS treated). If you decide it is an alias, those five products gain a PFAS escalation and a flag.

**Provisional grades:** The three graded products are provisional (see the Anomalies section of the audit log). The Advanced Hydration SPF 30 Sunscreen Spray and Baby Wash and Shampoo carry audit status needs review. The five former Use Caution grades are ungradable under change 3, so no Eucerin product is published as Use Caution. Do not publish any tier until the full INCI lists are captured.

**D8 (2026-10-16, one unknown ingredient):** Four products moved from ungradable to Use Caution, each on one unknown: Q10 Revitalize Night Cream (Q10), Daily Hydration Lotion (helianthus annuus (sunflower) seed oil), Eucerin Baby Eczema Relief Hydrogel (Colloidal Oatmeal) and Baby Lotion (Dexpanthenol). One moved to Avoid (High Priority Swap): Moisture Boost Serum (Sodium Ascorbyl Phosphate), where the existing flags already set that tier. All four Use Caution moves rest on source text the audit notes mark as unresolved or conflicting, so they are provisional.

**Change 3 (2026-10-17, explicit exception):** Five products moved from Use Caution to ungradable: Q10 Revitalize Night Cream, Daily Hydration Lotion with Broad Spectrum SPF 15, Daily Hydration Lotion, Eucerin Baby Eczema Relief Hydrogel and Baby Lotion. Each has audit status needs review. The rule as written (Use Caution with needs review) matches five products, not four. The fifth is the Daily Hydration Lotion with Broad Spectrum SPF 15, whose grade rests on one printed key ingredient, Octisalate (3.5), and which has no unknown ingredient. Its Octisalate flag is kept in the row. If the four-product reading was intended, remove that one entry from UNGRADABLE_EXCEPTIONS in _grader.py and regrade.
