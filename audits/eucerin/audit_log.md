## Anomalies

- Partial ingredient lists site-wide. Each product block prints key ingredients ("Learn more about the key ingredients of this product") or active ingredients (sunscreens and oatmeal products). No full INCI list was found on any product page or on the site's ingredients glossary (/our-research/ingredients is a definitions index, not per-product). Every tier is provisional.
- The site's own disclaimer says printed ingredients may differ from packaging. Packaging was not reviewed.
- Identical printed text across products: "Water, Glycerin, Urea" appears on four Advanced Repair SKUs (cream, lotion, hand cream, 8 oz tube).
- Product names on the product page (h1) differ from the catalog cards in places (for example "Gel-Cream", "Eye Cream", "Itch Relief Intensive Calming Lotion"). The h1 is used as product_name.
- The two Radiant Tone body products (Dark Spot Corrector Body Cream and Lotion) are tagged "NEW" on the catalog. No reformulation dates were visible.
- The site's catalog includes 48 products under /products (All Products), and the category pages (face-care, body, kid-baby-care, sun, skin-balance) contain no products outside that set.
