# Aquaphor Audit — Content Mining Report

**Date audited:** 2026-10-16 (regraded 2026-10-17 under practitioner decisions D1 to D6 and changes 1 to 3, with D7 and D8 removed, and the Puberty Safe zero-unknowns rule; flags are unchanged)
**Products audited:** 41 listed: 38 graded (5 Use Caution, 2 Avoid (Moderate), 31 Avoid (High Priority Swap)), 3 ungradable (no ingredient text)
**Source:** https://www.aquaphorus.com/ (product names and retailer-printed ingredient lists; sources per row in the audit log). aquaphor.com is an unrelated company and was not used.

**Read this first:** Each grade uses the known ingredients only. A printed ingredient the reference does not cover adds no flag and does not change the tier (2026-10-17, replacing D7 and D8), with one exception: a product cannot be Puberty Safe while any ingredient is unknown, so it grades Use Caution instead (2026-10-17 zero-unknowns rule). "Ungradable" now means only that the page shows no ingredient text. Most Aquaphor products contain base ingredients the reference does not list, such as Caprylic/Capric Triglyceride, Shea Butter, Castor Seed Oil, or Lanolin Alcohol. Those are excluded from the grade, so a Use Caution tier here can still rest on several unknowns. No Aquaphor product is Puberty Safe under the zero-unknowns rule. Partial flags are shown for ranking only.

## Section A — Brand-level callout

**Headline finding:** Of 41 Aquaphor products, 38 can now be graded from their known ingredients: 31 Avoid (High Priority Swap), 2 Avoid (Moderate) and 5 Use Caution. None is Puberty Safe. The other 3 have no ingredient text. All five graded Use Caution products have 5 to 14 unknown ingredients, so those grades are the weakest in the brand. Bisabolol, a category 4 flag, appears in the partial flags of 27 of 41 products.

**Tier distribution (41 products):**
- Puberty Safe: 0 (0%)
- Use Caution: 5 (12%)
- Avoid (Mild): 0 (0%)
- Avoid (Moderate): 2 (5%)
- Avoid (High Priority Swap): 31 (76%)
- Ungradable, counted separately: 3 (7%), all with no ingredient text

**Single most-flagged product:** Aquaphor Lip Protectant + SPF 30 and Aquaphor Lip Protectant + SPF (Dual Pack) — 7 partial flags each: Petrolatum (category 1), Avobenzone, Octinoxate, Octisalate, Octocrylene, Oxybenzone (category 2), and BHT. Both grade Avoid (High Priority Swap) on their known ingredients.

**Cleanest product (if any):** Aquaphor Lip Repair Stick Naturally Inspired, Aloe Vera and Coconut single packs — 0 partial flags each. The Coconut pack is Use Caution with 9 unknowns excluded (it was Puberty Safe before the zero-unknowns rule), and the Aloe Vera pack is Use Caution with 11 excluded. Zero flags here does not mean cleared.

**Content angle for Section A:**
"I graded 38 of the 41 Aquaphor products from the ingredients the reference covers. Most come out Avoid (High Priority Swap) on the listed actives, and most also share base ingredients the reference does not cover, which are excluded from the grade. Only three pages show no ingredient list at all." Frame it as a coverage gap.

## Section B — Pattern callouts

**Most common flagged ingredient:** Bisabolol — in 27 of 41 products. It is a full category 4 flag under D3, because no chamomile oil or extract is printed beside it in any of these lists. Next: Petrolatum (18), Mineral Oil (14), Phenoxyethanol (9), Sodium Cetearyl Sulfate (8).

**Most common risk category:** Category 4 (Allergies, Irritation, Corrosion) and Category 1 (Carcinogen), each flagged in 29 of 41 products (partial flags).

**"Clean marketing, dirty formula" finds:**
- The two "Naturally Inspired" lip sticks are the only products with zero partial flags. Each still has 9 to 11 ingredients the reference does not cover, which are excluded from the grade. On their known ingredients, both are Use Caution. The Coconut pack was Puberty Safe until the zero-unknowns rule.
- The Lip Protectant + SPF products carry seven partial flags, including sunscreen filters. A lip product with SPF is marketed differently from a plain lip balm, and the flags reflect that.

**Repeated naming patterns:**
- Bisabolol appears in 27 products, every time as a full flag under D3.
- Ceramide AP, NP and NG appear in 6 to 7 products each. Under D4 they are LOW TOX and are not flagged.
- Lanolin Alcohol appears in 14 products. The reference does not list it, so it is unknown under D4.

**Content angles for Section B:**
1. "Bisabolol is in 27 of the 41 Aquaphor products I checked. None of the printed lists names chamomile beside it, so the reference's category 4 flag applies in full." Use the caveat.
2. "The two Naturally Inspired lip sticks have zero flags and still have 9 to 11 ingredients the reference does not cover. Zero flags is not the same as cleared." Keep the framing.
3. "Lanolin Alcohol is in 14 of the 41 products. The reference does not list it, so I log it as unknown rather than assume it is low tox."

## Raw data summary

**Unknown ingredients (manual review queue):** 48 distinct names. Most frequent: Caprylic/Capric Triglyceride (16 products), Butyrospermum Parkii (Shea) Butter (16), Ricinus Communis (Castor) Seed Oil (16), Ceresin (14), Lanolin Alcohol (13), Hydrogenated Castor Oil (12), Octyldodecanol (11), Tocopheryl Acetate (9). All 233 unknown occurrences are excluded from the grade. The full deduplicated list is in the Regrade section of `aquaphor_audit_log.md`.

**Ingredient text not captured:** Aquaphor Diaper Rash Cream (3oz.), Aquaphor Hand Mask, and Aquaphor Healing Ointment (2x.3.5oz).

**Naming decisions:** Lanolin Alcohol (D4) is still the largest naming decision for this brand. It is excluded from the grade like any other unknown, but if it is added to the reference, every product that lists it may change tier.

**D7 and D8 removed (2026-10-17):** 36 products that were ungradable on unknowns are now graded from their known ingredients: 31 Avoid (High Priority Swap), 4 Use Caution and 1 Puberty Safe. The two Baby Wash & Shampoo listings were already Avoid (Moderate) and keep that tier. See the Grading on known ingredients section of `aquaphor_audit_log.md`. Under the zero-unknowns rule (see the end of this report), the Puberty Safe grade in that list is now Use Caution.

**Changes 1 to 3 (2026-10-17):** No Aquaphor product has polysorbate or White Petrolatum USP, and none is on the Eucerin exception list, so changes 1 to 3 did not move any Aquaphor tier. The Avoid (Moderate) count is unchanged at 2, and the three no-text products remain ungradable.

**Puberty Safe requires zero unknowns (2026-10-17):** One product moved from Puberty Safe to Use Caution: Aquaphor® Lip Repair Stick Naturally Inspired Coconut Single Pack (9 unknowns). No Aquaphor product is Puberty Safe now. The before-and-after counts are in `aquaphor_audit_log.md`.
