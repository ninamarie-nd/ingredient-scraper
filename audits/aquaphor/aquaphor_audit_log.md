## Scrape method

- Product pages were opened in the browser pane (tab-5) to confirm layout and to check the cookie banner. The cookie banner was not accepted; no consent was given.
- The ingredient block on each page was read verbatim from the page DOM (the `.ingredients-wrapper` element) with a same-origin fetch of all 41 product URLs. Text was not paraphrased.
- Every product page shows one ingredient block only. For cosmetic formulas it shows the first two or three INCI names (e.g. "Water, Glycerin, Caprylic/Capric Triglyceride"). For OTC-format products it shows the "Active Ingredients" list (e.g. "Petrolatum (41%)"). The "View all" link in the Ingredients section goes to the same text and expands nothing. No full INCI list was found on any of the 41 pages.
- All 41 are therefore recorded as `needs review`. Tiers are provisional. The verbatim text is what the site shows, not the full label.

## Grading method

- Grader: copied from `audits/briogeo/_grader.py` into this folder with paths corrected. Three runtime overrides were applied so the rubric and reference govern grading:
  1. Low-tox status comes only from the reference PDF's LOW TOX entries. The grader's hardcoded LOW_TOX list (which included water, glycerin, urea, hydrocortisone and several esters/oils) was dropped; those ingredients are not in the reference and are routed to manual review.
  2. The generic "ends in oil / butter / extract" skip now runs only for ingredients that are not in the reference. This keeps mineral oil (category 1) from being skipped.
  3. "Zinc Oxide" is matched to the reference entry "zinc oxide (non-nano)" (low tox in creams).
- Lip Protectant + SPF products (typed as sunscreen) were escalated to High Priority Swap manually: petrolatum (category 1) in a leave-on product.
- Hand-checked 3 products against the reference: Lip Repair Stick + Sunscreen (four category-2 filters, Avoid Mild), Lip Protectant + SPF 30 (petrolatum category 1 plus five category-2 filters, escalated), and Ointment Body Spray 3.7oz (petrolatum and mineral oil category 1, leave-on escalation).
- Product types: 12 Puberty Scanner types used. Leave-on skincare for creams, lotions, ointments, balms, masks, body spray; lip product for lip repair sticks and lip repair (no SPF); sunscreen for SPF lip products; body wash/shampoo/conditioner for baby washes.

## Anomalies

- The brand site shows partial ingredient lists only (see Scrape method). This is the main limit on the audit. Re-grade against packaging before any output is published.
- The site's own FAQ says the ingredients on the website and packaging can differ, and recommends checking packaging.
- The Ointment Body Spray is listed as both 3.7 oz and 6.2 oz with the same displayed ingredients. Not a conflict, but a reminder that sizes share formulas.
- Diaper Rash Cream and Paste show zinc oxide at 15% and 40%. The reference lists zinc oxide (non-nano) as low tox in creams, but nano status is not stated on the page; a nano form would change the grade.
- Review counts and ratings on the catalog were not used.
