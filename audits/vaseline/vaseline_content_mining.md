# Vaseline Audit — Content Mining Report

**Date audited:** 2026-10-16
**Products audited:** 36 graded of 52 enumerated (16 had no ingredient text on the product page and are not graded)
**Source:** https://www.vaseline.com/us/en/products.html (US site)

---

## Section A — Brand-level callout

**Headline finding:** Of 36 Vaseline products with ingredient lists captured, all 36 grade Avoid (High Priority Swap), because the reference classifies petrolatum, mineral oil and paraffin as category 1 and every graded product contains one of them in a leave-on form.

**Tier distribution (as the rubric grades them):**
- Puberty Safe: 0 (0%)
- Use Caution: 0 (0%)
- Avoid (Mild): 0 (0%)
- Avoid (Moderate): 0 (0%)
- Avoid (High Priority Swap): 36 (100%)

**Sensitivity check (not the rubric tier, for internal use):** The High Priority grade rests on two escalations. Removing the carcinogen-in-leave-on escalation moves the distribution to 25 High Priority, 3 Avoid (Moderate), 8 Use Caution (69% / 8% / 22%). Removing the PFAS escalation as well moves it to 23 High Priority, 5 Avoid (Moderate), 8 Use Caution (64% / 14% / 22%). Keep this in mind before publishing any tier claim: the single biggest driver is one reference classification (petrolatum, mineral oil and paraffin as category 1).

**Single most-flagged product:** Vaseline Intensive Care Cocoa Body Butter — 18 flags. Flags include petrolatum, paraffin, fragrance (parfum), phenoxyethanol, methylparaben, propylparaben, several dimethicone and siloxane entries, and multiple PEG and -eth ethoxylates.

**Cleanest product (if any):** Vaseline Healing Jelly Original — High Priority Swap. Its full ingredient list is a single line, "White Petrolatum Usp (100%)", so it carries one flag. Six other products also carry one flag (four Lip Therapy minis, Lip Therapy Original Stick, Lip Therapy Advanced Healing Tube). Every one of them is High Priority because of that single petrolatum entry.

**Content angle for Section A:**
"I scanned 36 Vaseline products. The reference flags petrolatum, mineral oil and paraffin as category 1 carcinogens, so every one of them lands in the top tier. The brand's own single-ingredient jelly is the cleanest on flag count and still lands there." Keep it to the data; no cause language.

## Section B — Pattern callouts

**Most common flagged ingredient:** Petrolatum — appears in 32 of 36 graded products (plus mineral oil in 8 and paraffin in 3).

**Most common risk category:** Category 1 (Carcinogen) — flagged in 36 of 36 graded products, driven by petrolatum, mineral oil, paraffin and fragrance. By product coverage, the next most common are category 2 (Endocrine Disruption, 28 products), category 4 (Allergies, Irritation, 27), and category 8 (Respiratory Irritant, 27).

**"Clean marketing, dirty formula" finds:**
- **Vaseline Healing Jelly Original.** The page describes it as "100% pure and triple-purified," "hypoallergenic" and "fragrance-free," and cites a National Eczema Association seal. Its only listed ingredient is White Petrolatum USP (100%). It grades High Priority Swap on that one entry.
- **Vaseline Advanced Repair Unscented Lotion.** The "unscented" label means no fragrance flag, but the product still has 7 flags: dimethicone, petrolatum, mineral oil, PEG-100 stearate, phenoxyethanol, methylparaben and triethanolamine. The printed list is also cut off at "PROPYLP", so the true count may be higher (see Raw data summary).
- **Vaseline Lip Therapy Original Mini.** Ingredients are "Petrolatum, Yellow 5 Lake." It has one flag and is still High Priority, because the single petrolatum entry is a category 1 leave-on flag.

**Repeated naming patterns:**
- Every graded product (36 of 36) contains petrolatum, mineral oil or paraffin, each category 1 in the reference.
- Phenoxyethanol appears in 18 of 36; dimethicone in 18 of 36.
- Methylparaben or propylparaben appears in 13 of 36 lotions, creams and sprays.
- PEG or "-eth" ethoxylates (PEG-100 stearate, Steareth-75, Ceteth-20 and similar) appear in 10 of 36 products.
- Fragrance (parfum) is listed in 26 of 36 products.
- Lip products: all 10 graded lip products use the same petrolatum base. The 5 remaining lip SKUs have no ingredient text on the page.

**Content angles for Section B:**
1. "One ingredient, same grade." Set the 1-flag lip minis against the 13-flag lotions. Same tier, very different ingredient lists.
2. "Unscented is not the same as unflagged." Use the Advanced Repair Unscented Lotion: no fragrance flag, but 7 flags.
3. "100% pure, still High Priority." Use the Healing Jelly Original page copy against its single-ingredient list and tier.

## Raw data summary

**Unknown ingredients across the audit (manual review queue):** 60 unique names. They are not flagged in the totals. The full list, with the product count for each, is in `vaseline_audit_log.md` under "Manual review queue." The largest group is colorants (Yellow 5 Lake, Red 30 Lake, Red 7 Lake, CI 19140 variants), then fatty-acid esters and waxes (glycol stearate, microcrystalline wax, C20-40 alkyl stearate, stearamide AMP), then botanical extracts and oils not yet in the reference.

**Reformulation flags:** None observed. Two observations: (1) the Advanced Repair Unscented Lotion ingredient list is cut off at "PROPYLP" on the page and in the data layer, so the page itself is incomplete; (2) the Rosy Lips Stick carnauba entry prints as "Camauba," which is kept as printed.

**Grading caveats for review before any publication:**
- Petrolatum, mineral oil and paraffin are category 1 in the reference. This single classification sets the tier for all 36 graded products. Confirm it reflects the reference's intent before posting any tier claim.
- Dimethicone (and other silicone entries) carry a "PFAS treated" note in the reference. That note triggers the PFAS escalation in 25 products: 18 with silicones, and 7 with iron oxides or titanium dioxide (the shimmer gel oils, the jelly stick, the Lip Therapy sticks and the Rosy Lips Tin).
- Titanium dioxide is exempt in lotion and cream bases, and flagged in the Lip Therapy Rosy Lips Tin, the shimmer gel oils and the jelly stick.
- Mica is not flagged, because it is never the first ingredient in these products.
