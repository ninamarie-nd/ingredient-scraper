# Jergens Audit — Content Mining Report

**Date audited:** 2026-10-16 (regraded 2026-10-17 under practitioner decisions D1 to D6 and change 2, polysorbate flags, with D7 and D8 removed)
**Products audited:** 38 catalog items: 31 graded (all Avoid (High Priority Swap)), 7 ungradable (no ingredient text)
**Source:** https://us.jergens.com/ (Shop All and the category collections)

**Read this first:** Each grade uses the known ingredients only. A printed ingredient the reference does not cover adds no flag and does not change the tier (2026-10-17, replacing D7 and D8). "Ungradable" now means only that the page shows no ingredient text. Bundles and multi-shade pages merge several ingredient lists into one block, so their tiers and partial flags are not product grades. Do not publish bundle figures until each bundle's single-product lists are captured.

## Section B — Pattern callouts

**Most common flagged ingredient:** Fragrance (no disclosure) — in the partial flags of 29 of 38 products. Next: Dimethicone (26), Ceteareth-20 (21), Phenoxyethanol (21), Mineral Oil (16), Methylparaben (15).

**Most common risk category:** Categories 1 (Carcinogen), 2 (Endocrine Disruption), 4 (Allergies, Irritation, Corrosion) and 5 (Microbiome-Busting) — each in the partial flags of 31 of 38 products.

**"Clean marketing, dirty formula" finds:**
- Natural Glow Wet Skin Moisturizer: the "Natural Glow" name, 15 partial flags, including Fragrance (no disclosure).
- Natural Glow Instant Sun Moisturizer + Bronzer: 14 partial flags, including Fragrance, Geraniol, and Limonene (natural components flagged in full because no source essential oil is listed).
- Ultra Healing Fragrance Free Moisturizer: the label says fragrance-free, but no ingredient text was captured, so the label cannot be checked from the data. Re-scrape it before any post.

**Repeated naming patterns:**
- Parabens are common. Methylparaben is in the partial flags of 15 products, and Ethylparaben is in others. Both are flagged under the paraben pattern (categories 2 and 4).
- Sodium Hydroxide (20 products) and Tocopheryl Acetate (20) are the most common unknowns. Neither is in the reference.

**Content angles for Section B:**
1. "Fragrance is in the printed list of 29 of the 38 Jergens products I checked." Note that bundle lists are merged, and that the count is partial.
2. "A product named 'Natural Glow' listed 15 partial flags. The name is marketing; the list is the part to read." Keep the wording neutral.

## Raw data summary

**Unknown ingredients (manual review queue):** 141 distinct names. Most frequent: Sodium Hydroxide (20 products), Tocopheryl Acetate (20), Stearic Acid (19), Arginine (14), Acrylates/C10-30 Alkyl Acrylate Crosspolymer (14), Butyrospermum Parkii (Shea) Butter (11), Ethylhexyl Isononanoate (10), Hydroxyacetophenone (10). All 451 unknown occurrences are excluded from the grade. The full deduplicated list is in the Regrade section of `jergens_audit_log.md`.

**Parser artifacts (not ingredients):** Bundle lists contain "||" separators, bracketed shade names (for example "[Glow Daily Moisturizer] Water"), repeated "FRAGRANCE INGREDIENTS:" blocks, and fragments such as "Caramel FRAGRANCE", "Ext. Violet 2", and "Ethylparaben With an illuminating HYDRALUCENCE blend". These stay unknown under D4 and are excluded from the grade, so the bundle tiers come from merged and partly unreadable lists. Recapture the single-product pages.

**Botanicals not in the reference:** Cocos Nucifera (Coconut) Water and Cocos Nucifera (Coconut) Fruit Extract are unknown under D4. The coconut oil entry is LOW TOX and is not flagged.

**Old files in this folder:** `_grader_jergens.py`, `_master.py`, `_save.py`, and `_master_list.json` predate the unified grader. `_grader_jergens.py` is superseded by `_grader.py` and was not run.

**D7 and D8 removed (2026-10-17):** The 31 products that were ungradable on unknowns are now graded from their known ingredients, all Avoid (High Priority Swap). See the Grading on known ingredients section of `jergens_audit_log.md`.

**Polysorbate flags (change 2, 2026-10-17):** Polysorbate 20 is flagged in Natural Glow Instant Sun Drops, and Polysorbate 60 in Natural Glow + Firming Daily Moisturizer, Natural Glow Daily Moisturizer, Gotta Glow Bundle and Daily Glow Bundle. Each product flagged under change 2 is already Avoid (High Priority Swap) on the known-ingredient grade, so the polysorbate flags do not change a tier here.
