# Jergens Audit — Content Mining Report

**Date audited:** 2026-10-16
**Products audited:** 38 catalog items listed on the US shop (31 graded, 7 not graded: no readable ingredient list or not a cosmetic)
**Source:** https://us.jergens.com/ (Shop All, plus the nine category collections in the Shop menu, all checked)

Grading uses the Puberty-Safe rubric as written, against `ref_compact.json`, the aliases file, and the 2026-10-09 addendum. Ingredient lists are verbatim from each product page. Bundles and multi-shade pages are graded as one union per product.

---

## Section A — Brand-level callout

**Headline finding:** Of the 31 Jergens products with a readable ingredient list, all 31 grade Avoid (High Priority Swap) under the rubric as written, and the escalations driving that are present in almost every formula (see sensitivity note below).

**Tier distribution (31 graded):**
- Puberty Safe: 0 (0%)
- Use Caution: 0 (0%)
- Avoid (Mild): 0 (0%)
- Avoid (Moderate): 0 (0%)
- Avoid (High Priority Swap): 31 (100%)

**Sensitivity (for Nina-Marie's review, not a regrade):** The High Priority tier comes from two automatic escalations. (1) "PFAS treated" is noted in the reference for dimethicone, which 26 of 31 formulas contain. (2) Category 1 entries, such as phenoxyethanol (21 of 31), mineral oil (16), petrolatum (14), and carbomer, trigger the "known carcinogen in leave-on" rule. Setting aside both escalations moves the grades to 21 High Priority and 10 Avoid (Moderate). No product grades lower than Moderate under any setting, so the tier spread is narrow regardless. Flag count is the more useful ranking within the catalog.

**Single most-flagged product:** Gotta Glow Bundle, 23 flags. It is a bundle of three formulas (sunscreen, instant sun mousse, daily moisturizer), so its count is a union across three ingredient lists. The most-flagged single product is Natural Glow Wet Skin Moisturizer, with 15 flags.

**Cleanest product (if any):** Sparkling Berry Shea Fusion Body Lotion, 4 flags (fragrance, dimethicone, ceteareth-20, phenoxyethanol). Still High Priority Swap under the rubric, because of the dimethicone PFAS note and phenoxyethanol.

**Content angle for Section A:**
"I checked every ingredient list on the Jergens US site. 31 products had one I could read, and all 31 land in the top tier of my rubric. The bundles stack up to 23 flagged ingredients." Keep the tier language to the rubric's wording and the flag counts, not a claim about the brand.

## Section B — Pattern callouts

**Most common flagged ingredient:** Fragrance (listed as "Fragrance" with no essential-oil breakdown in single products) — appears in 29 of 31 graded products. Next: dimethicone (26 of 31), ceteareth-20 (21 of 31), phenoxyethanol (21 of 31).

**Most common risk category:** A four-way tie at full coverage. Category 1 (Carcinogen), Category 2 (Endocrine Disruption), Category 4 (Allergies, Irritation), and Category 5 (Microbiome-Busting) each appear in 31 of 31 graded products. Category 8 (Respiratory irritant, e.g., fragrance and phenoxyethanol) appears in 30 of 31. Category 3 (Heavy Metals) appears in 9 of 31, through aluminum starch octenylsuccinate. Category 6 (Microplastics) appears in 26 of 31.

**"Clean marketing, dirty formula" finds:**
- *Ultra Healing Fragrance Free Moisturizer* is named fragrance free, but its ingredients panel on the live page reads only "NEED COPY." Nothing can be graded, so the claim cannot be checked against a list.
- *Cherry Swirl Shea Fusion* (the page is titled Cherry Shea Fusion Body Lotion) carries a "paraben free" line in its feel-good copy, but its ingredients panel also reads "NEED COPY." The claim cannot be checked.
- The "Natural Glow" line name appears on 8 single products. All 8 grade High Priority Swap, with 6 to 15 flags. Seven of the eight contain fragrance. "Natural" in the line name does not change the grade. This is a rubric-level point: natural framing is not protective.

**Repeated naming patterns:**
- "-eth" ethoxylates and PEG-series ingredients appear in 27 of 31 products. Ceteareth-20 alone is in 21 of 31.
- Parabens (methylparaben, ethylparaben, propylparaben) appear in 16 of 31 products.
- Fatty alcohols (cetearyl alcohol, cetyl alcohol, behenyl alcohol) appear in 25 of 31 formulas. Under the rubric they are not flagged.
- Quat-type conditioning agents show up in one product only (polyquaternium-37 in the Natural Glow Wet Skin Moisturizer). This is a judgment call; see the audit log.

**Content angles for Section B:**
1. "Fragrance is in 29 of the 31 readable Jergens formulas." This is the single most repeatable finding in the catalog.
2. "Parabens are in half the catalog." Sixteen of 31 graded formulas list at least one paraben.
3. "Same name, two formulas." The Ultra Healing Moisturizer standalone page lists no fragrance and no parabens. Its component inside the Best Sellers Bundle lists fragrance, methylparaben, and propylparaben. Same product name, different ingredient list.
