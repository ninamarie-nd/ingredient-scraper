## Grading decisions (for review)

- Reference first. The template ran a hardcoded LOW_TOX list before the reference, which marked carbomer LOW even though the reference flags it [1,4,6]. This run uses the reference (plus the addendum) first, and the template's LOW list only for names absent from the reference (see manual review queue above).
- PFAS escalation applies to reference entries whose note says "PFAS treated" (dimethicone, cyclopentasiloxane, and others). Dimethicone appears in 26 of 31 graded formulas. Literal application produces High Priority Swap for every formula. Without the PFAS escalation the tier is unchanged, because the cat-1 escalation also applies everywhere.
- Carcinogen-in-leave-on escalation applies to direct reference or addendum category-1 entries. Phenoxyethanol, mineral oil, petrolatum, carbomer, and polyacrylamide carry category 1 in the reference. Applied to all 31 graded formulas (leave-on). Without this escalation: 27 High Priority Swap, 4 Avoid (Moderate). Without both: 21 High Priority Swap, 10 Avoid (Moderate).
- Fragrance listed (with or without breakdown) is a full flag and triggers Avoid (Moderate). The rubric's soft rule applies only to essential-oil fragrance with EO sources disclosed. Here the breakdown is synthetic aroma components, not essential oils.
- Pattern extension: polyquaternium-37 (Natural Glow Wet Skin Moisturizer) is flagged as a quaternary ammonium compound [2], inferred. The rubric says quats follow the "-nium" pattern; polyquaternium is not named there. This is a judgment call.
- Bundles and multi-shade pages graded as one union per product. The bundle tier is a union across component formulas, not an average.
- Bundles treated as leave-on for the cat-1 escalation (all components are leave-on).
- Fatty alcohols (cetearyl, cetyl, behenyl, stearyl, C14-22) exempted per the rubric.
- The standalone sunscreen is graded on its own. The bundle version of the sunscreen is graded inside the Gotta Glow and Daily Glow bundle unions.
